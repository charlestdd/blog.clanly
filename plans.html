Paid Plans

What are paid plans? How do they work?

Buying a paid plan supports our business and also brings users more benefits.

Clanly Budget

This is the cheaper plan compared to Clanly Plus and Ultimate but it comes with half the features.

Pricing:

  • $2.50 USD

Perks:

  • Increased Friend Limit: +10
  • Increased Server Limit: +10
  • Increased Logged in devices: +1
  • Profile Customizatin Bundle A
  • Your Region is still displayed to everyone.

Clanly Plus

This is the balanced plan for users on a budget!

Pricing:

  • $5.00 USD

Perks:

  • Increased Friend Limit: +20
  • Increased Server Limit: +20
  • Increased Logged in devices: +2
  • Your Region is displayed to everyone.
  • Profile Customization Bundle B

Clanly Ultimate

This is the ultimate plan for users on a budget!

Pricing:

  • $9.99 USD

Perks:

  • Increased Friend Limit: +50
  • Increased Server Limit: +50
  • Increased Logged in devices: +3
  • Your Region display can be toggled on or off.
  • Profile Customization Bundle B
