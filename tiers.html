Tiers

What are tiers? How do they work?

Tiering is one of the ways to identify a user's credibility. For example, if you're looking to friend someone, you can refer to his/her/their/its tier level. In this article, we talk about the available tiers a user could have.

Sprout Tier

The Sprout Tier is the basic plan that a user owns when one creates an account.

Requirements:

  • Verified Email
  • Verified Phone Number

Account Limitiations

  • Friend Limit: 20
  • Server Limit: 30
  • Logged in devices: 1
  • Your Region is displayed to everyone.
